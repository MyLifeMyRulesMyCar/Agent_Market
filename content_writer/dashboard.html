03 Publishing Workflow
1
Open the draft file
Each draft is saved as drafts/YYYY-MM-DD_keyword-slug.md. Open it in any markdown editor.
2
Fill in placeholders
Replace [PRODUCT_NAME] → actual product name, [SHOP_LINK] → your store URL, [PRICE_USD] → current price, [DOCS_LINK] → documentation URL.
3
Review quality flags
Articles with ⚠ flags need a human review pass — check word count, verify no hallucinated specs, and confirm CTAs are working.
4
Publish
Copy to your CMS (WordPress, Ghost, Webflow, etc.). The front matter YAML has SEO metadata pre-filled.
